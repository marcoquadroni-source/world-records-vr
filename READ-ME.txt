IMPLEMENTATION
Procedural architecture with fluted wall details, recessed bronze seams and translucent skylight. Multi-scale original stone color and bump maps, clear-coated physical floor material, softened lighting contrast, reduced overexposure, generated reflection environment, shadows and ACES tone mapping.
Four locally bundled 2048x1024 image maps from Solar System Scope replace the earlier procedural planet patterns. Earth has a separate cloud shell and approximate atmospheric rim shader. Moon albedo is also used for approximate bump relief; this is not measured topography. Contact darkening uses an original radial gradient approximation, not computed global illumination.
This is a more naturalistic real-time 3D prototype, not full photorealism. Planet image maps are edited composites: the provider notes saturated color and fictional terrain in unmapped areas. Sphere shapes do not reproduce flattening. Museum stone is procedural, not a scanned surface. No ray tracing or baked global illumination.
Desktop: choose exhibits, drag view, arrow keys, help, diameter comparison and VR availability feedback.
VR code: controller rays, trigger-select plaques, fixed teleport destinations, right-stick 30-degree snap turning about the viewer. Plaques carry diameters in-world. DOM controls are hidden during XR. System menu ends session.
No hand tracking, MR, narration, eight-language support, store integration or complete record catalogue is claimed. This iteration is Swedish only.
