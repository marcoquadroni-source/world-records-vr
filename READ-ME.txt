RUN
Serve this entire folder with a static HTTP server. No build step. All runtime modules, textures and notices are local files in the same folder. HTTPS is required for headset WebXR away from localhost. There are no analytics, paid APIs, subscriptions or runtime asset CDN requests. Only optional source/license links navigate externally.

VISUAL TRUTH
Museum architecture and the fallback stone texture are procedural. The hall adds the Poly Haven Marble 01 floor maps, Studio Small 03 HDR reflections and Painted Wooden Bench under CC0. See RIGHTS.json for per-file provenance. Planet imagery uses surface maps and real-time shading on spheres. Saturn rings use their own original ring geometry and licensed texture; ring dimensions are schematic and excluded from the body-diameter statistic. Venus shows its cloud cover. Earth clouds and their offset shadows are composited in the surface shader. No measured lunar topography, true atmosphere simulation, ray tracing, baked global illumination or scientific orbital simulation is claimed.
Outside the solar-system section, 32 exhibits use original procedural sculptures. Saturn V uses the NASA model with a procedural fallback. They are not photorealistic, anatomical, engineering or fossil reconstructions. Human figures are generic measurement figures, not portraits. Similar shapes share parameterized model construction. No third-party human likeness, vehicle logo or downloaded animal model is included.

SCALE
Default exhibition models are each individually fitted to a display podium; their apparent sizes are not directly comparable. Compare sets the three displayed measurements to a common metres-to-scene-unit ratio using each model's measurement axis. Heights, lengths, shoulder heights and wingspans can differ: the panel identifies the dimension. Saturn body diameter excludes ring span. Planet values are stored in metres and displayed in kilometres. These are miniatures, not life-size scenes. Pages with only one or two items have only that many models.
